# TODO — extra-deck manual research (2026-08-17)

## Motivation
Deck manuals under-document the Extra Deck. In modern Yu-Gi-Oh! the Extra Deck is a large
part of piloting skill and differs per deck, so an agent piloting from the manual plays
weaker than the deck's real ceiling. **Hard rule from the owner: no guessing.** Every
when/why claim must come from a real human source (deck profiles, YouTube transcripts,
community writeups) and be cited in the deck's `sources` field.

Proof case: `Chaos Angel` in shadow-spectre-endymion was entirely unmentioned. The main
deck runs ZERO Tuners, so intuition says it is unsummonable — it is actually live via its
"treat 1 LIGHT or DARK monster you control as a Tuner" clause. Exactly the kind of thing
research turns up and intuition does not.
