# Strategy: control and trade

Set monsters face-down when the opponent has bigger attackers on board; keep at
least one trap set (Trap Hole, Just Desserts, Reinforcements, Castle Walls,
Waboku) before ending your turn. Use Fissure / Dark Hole to clear before pushing
damage. Hold your boss monster (Blue-Eyes, Summoned Skull) until you have the
tributes and the opponent has no visible way to punish (check their set S/T
count and the "unseen" pool). Before attacking into a face-down, use the unseen
pool to judge whether it could be Man-Eater Bug or Hane-Hane; if it could be and
you cannot afford the loss, attack with a monster you can spare or pass.
