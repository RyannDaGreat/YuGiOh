# Strategy: aggressive beatdown

Summon your biggest monster face-up each turn and attack whenever the numbers
favor you. Set Trap Hole / Waboku when you have them. Tribute-summon Summoned
Skull / Dark Magician / Gaia / Blue-Eyes as soon as you can protect the tributes
for a turn. Attack face-down monsters with your weakest attacker that still
beats common flip-effect stats (Man-Eater Bug 450/600, Hane-Hane 450/500). Push
damage: a direct attack now beats a bigger board later.
